FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

What is STRIKTEK?

STRIKTEK is a research and paper-trading platform for Indian market strategies. You describe a strategy in plain English, it becomes exact rules, and a deterministic engine backtests them on real one-minute market data and paper-trades them live.

Does STRIKTEK place real trades?

No. It is research and paper trading only. There is no order endpoint, AI output can never place an order, and paper orders pass a deterministic risk engine with a kill switch.

Which markets does it cover?

Today: intraday NIFTY and BANKNIFTY options (buying calls and puts) and daily delivery strategies on NIFTY 50 stocks. Option selling and hedged multi-leg strategies are on the roadmap.

Where does the data come from?

From licensed broker market data: one-minute index, INDIA VIX and futures candles and expired option contracts going back up to seven years. Real and simulated data are never mixed, and every report states its data source and option-pricing mode.

How does the AI work?

The AI only translates your description into a strategy specification and can propose variants to test. Code decides everything else: the rules are validated, the engine runs them, and whether a variant is accepted is decided by walk-forward results, Deflated Sharpe and a minimum number of trades.

How are backtests kept honest?

Signals form on a closed candle and fill at the next candle's open, a candle that touches both stop and target counts as a stop, costs and slippage are charged on every trade, lot sizes and expiries follow the exchange's history, and a held-back period is never read by sweeps or the AI.

Is STRIKTEK investment advice?

No. It is a research and paper-trading tool. Past or simulated results do not predict future results, and most people who buy options lose money.
