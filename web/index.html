How it works

Data, then analysis, then paper.
Always in that order.

  1. 01

    Describe it

    Type or say your idea: “buy the ATM call when EMA 9 crosses EMA 21 above VWAP, stop 20%”. The AI drafts the rules and lists every assumption it made.

  2. 02

    Read the exact rules

    Code writes back what will run, in plain English. Change a word, a number or the JSON itself. What you read is exactly what the engine executes.

  3. 03

    Backtest on real data

    Years of 1-minute index, VIX and futures candles, with real expired option prices where stored. Costs, slippage and lot sizes included.

  4. 04

    Validate, then paper-trade

    Walk-forward and a held-back period decide whether it survives. Then run it live on paper, behind a risk engine and a kill switch.

Honest testing

Backtests that would rather disappoint you than flatter you.

Most backtests look great because they peek at the future or skip the costs. These are the rules STRIKTEK's engine never breaks.

  • No look-ahead. A signal forms on a closed candle and fills at the next candle's open.
  • The stop wins a tie. If one candle touches both the stop and the target, it counts as a stop.
  • Every cost, every trade. Brokerage, STT, exchange charges, GST, stamp duty, spread and slippage.
  • Labelled sources. Real and simulated data are never mixed, and every report says which it used.
  • AI proposes, code decides. The model drafts and suggests; validation, testing and acceptance are code.
  • Same inputs, same answer. An identical run on unchanged data loads instantly from the stored result.

FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

What is STRIKTEK?

STRIKTEK is a research and paper-trading platform for intraday NIFTY and BANKNIFTY option buying. You describe a strategy in plain English, it becomes exact rules, and a deterministic engine backtests them on real one-minute market data and paper-trades them live.

Does STRIKTEK place real trades?

No. It is research and paper trading only. There is no order endpoint, AI output can never place an order, and paper orders pass a deterministic risk engine with a kill switch.

Which markets does it cover?

Intraday option buying on NIFTY and BANKNIFTY (calls and puts), and daily long-only delivery strategies on NIFTY 50 stocks.

Where does the data come from?

From the broker (Fyers): one-minute index, INDIA VIX and futures candles and expired option contracts going back up to seven years. Real and simulated data are never mixed, and every report states its data source and option-pricing mode.

How does the AI work?

The AI only translates your description into a strategy specification and can propose variants to test. Code decides everything else: the rules are validated, the engine runs them, and whether a variant is accepted is decided by walk-forward results, Deflated Sharpe and a minimum number of trades.

How are backtests kept honest?

Signals form on a closed candle and fill at the next candle's open, a candle that touches both stop and target counts as a stop, costs and slippage are charged on every trade, lot sizes and expiries follow the exchange's history, and a held-back period is never read by sweeps or the AI.

Is STRIKTEK investment advice?

No. It is a research and paper-trading tool. Past or simulated results do not predict future results, and most people who buy options lose money.
